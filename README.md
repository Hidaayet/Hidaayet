Biomedical Engineering · Computational Methods · Medical Technology

I am a biomedical engineering student interested in understanding how engineering can be used to investigate biological systems, interpret medical data, and build technologies for healthcare.

My work sits at the intersection of biomedical engineering, computation, and research.

I am particularly drawn to problems where the biology is complex, the data is imperfect, and engineering can turn that complexity into something measurable, interpretable, or useful.

What I am exploring

My current interests span several connected areas:

Biomedical signals and neural engineering
Understanding physiological signals and developing computational methods for extracting meaningful information from them.

Medical imaging
Working with medical images not only as pictures, but as quantitative representations of anatomy and physiology.

Biomedical artificial intelligence
Exploring how machine learning can be applied to medical and biological data while paying attention to evaluation, interpretability, and the limitations of the data.

Computational biology
Using computational models to investigate biological mechanisms and problems that are difficult to study directly.

Biomedical systems
Connecting algorithms and data with the physical systems, sensors, and embedded technologies through which biomedical measurements are actually acquired.

How I approach projects

I am less interested in applying a model simply because it is available.

I want to understand the problem first:

             -->What is being measured?
             -->What does the data actually represent?
             -->What assumptions does the method make?
             -->What can the result tell us, and what can it not tell us?

From there, I try to build the complete chain:

biomedical problem → data → processing → model or system → evaluation → interpretation

My repositories are therefore intended to document not only the final result, but the reasoning and engineering decisions behind it.

Selected work

This profile contains projects across biomedical signals, medical imaging, computational biology, and biomedical AI.

Some are experiments exploring a specific method. Others are being developed into more complete engineering projects.

The goal is not to accumulate projects, but to progressively build the ability to take a biomedical problem from concept to reproducible technical implementation.
